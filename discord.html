The Discord.

The server address gets you into the world. Discord is where you ask for access, reach staff, report something, and talk to the people you build beside.

Open the invite

discord.gg/SMRS6skKYY

Request whitelist access

Only staff can add players. Ask in Discord, explain who you are, and complete the verification process they walk you through.

Report griefing or theft

Reported incidents are taken seriously and reviewed with whatever evidence is available. Bring details and timing when you report.

Ask about a rollback

If a verified server-side problem cost you blocks or items, staff need a report with the timing and any evidence you have.

A note on where things happen. This website is information only. It cannot whitelist you, take a report, or tell you who is online — all of that runs through the staff team in Discord. Links to Discord open in a new tab because it is an external service.
