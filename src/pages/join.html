Getting in

Join the world.

RenderFrost is whitelisted, so there are two real steps: get approved by staff, then connect. Nothing happens automatically, and nothing can be done by this website on the server's behalf.

Step 01

Join the Discord and ask a staff member

This is where access starts. Tell staff who you are and that you want to join. They will tell you what verification they need from you.

Open Discord
Step 02

Complete verification and wait for approval

Staff review the request and make the decision. Only authorised staff can add you to the whitelist — a personal invitation from another player is not access, and neither is knowing the address.

Step 03

Connect with the server address

Launch Minecraft Java Edition, add a server, and use the address below. There is no separate port to enter.

play.renderfrost.com
Step 04

Read the rules before your first session

Joining means agreeing to follow them and to cooperate with staff. The twenty rules take a few minutes to read and cover everything from griefing to how rollbacks are decided.

Read the rules

After you join

Two waiting periods worth knowing.

Both exist to keep the community trusted and steady, and neither can be shortened by anyone.

Day 15

You can recommend a player

After 15 days from your first login, you may ask staff to consider someone you would like to invite. They still complete verification, and meeting the waiting period does not guarantee approval.

Day 30

You become eligible for an Elytra gift

Established members can receive one Elytra from the staff team once they have completed 30 days, provided the conditions staff set are met. It never overrides the three-Elytra ownership limit.

What this page cannot do

There is no application form, account system or automatic signup here. This website can only display the server's status. It cannot add you to the whitelist, check your application, or confirm which version is currently supported. Every one of those goes through the staff team in Discord.
