# The RenderFrost code.

Twenty rules in five sections, built on trust, fairness, creativity and
respect. By playing on RenderFrost SMP you agree to follow them and to
cooperate with the staff team.

## Community Conduct & Safety

### Be Kind & Respectful

Every player deserves to feel welcome and respected within the RenderFrost community.

Prohibited behaviour

- Hate speech, discriminatory remarks and slurs.

- Harassment, targeted abuse or deliberate humiliation.

- Excessive vulgarity.

- Any conduct intended to make another player's experience hostile.

**Remember:** disagreements are normal, but personal attacks are strictly unacceptable.

### Protect Personal Information

Permanent ban

Strictly prohibited

- Doxxing.

- Sharing another player's private or real-world details.

- Threatening real-world harm.

**Penalty:** immediate permanent ban.

### No Advertising or Spam

Mute

- Do not advertise other servers, websites or external services in chat, on signs or in books.

- Repeated chat spam, flooding or disruptive messaging will result in a mute.

### Respect the Staff’s Decisions

Final decisions

- Moderation decisions are final.

- If you wish to contest a punishment, submit an appeal through the official support or ticket channel.

Arguing in public chat or harassing staff will result in additional penalties.

## Property, Trading & Combat

### No Griefing or Stealing

Every player's property, builds and possessions must be respected — even if a base is unattended or a container is unlocked.

Strictly prohibited

- Breaking, modifying or damaging another player's blocks, builds, farms or infrastructure without permission.

- Stealing items from chests, barrels, shulker boxes or other storage.

### Respect Private Storage & Restricted Areas

- Do not enter another player's private storage room without explicit permission.

Staff-only areas

Entering staff-only storage halls, accessing restricted containers or taking staff materials is strictly prohibited and is treated as **severe griefing or theft**.

### No Scamming or Fraudulent Trading

- Scamming other players in trades, player shops or item exchanges is strictly prohibited.

- All trades are considered final once both parties confirm the exchange.

### No Unconsented PvP or Player Harm

Consent required

PvP is disabled by default and permitted **only** when all involved players explicitly agree.

Prohibited behaviour

- Attacking players without consent.

- Trapping players in enclosed spaces.

- Using lava, dripstone or other hazards to endanger others.

- Engineering "accidental" deaths.

Consent to one fight does not grant permission for future or unrelated attacks.

### Do Not Interrupt Ongoing Friendly PvP

Jail time

When players are engaged in an agreed-upon friendly PvP session, do not run into the fight, obstruct the participants or distract them.

**Penalty:** intentional disruption may result in several hours of jail time.

### Limits on Explosives & Channeling Tridents

Explosives

TNT, creepers and end crystals must **not** be used near other players' builds or the Spawn area — even during consent-based PvP.

Channeling tridents

Do not use Channeling-enchanted tridents near another player's base or structures during thunderstorms.

Damage caused "as a joke" is treated as griefing and is not eligible for staff repair.

## Fair Play, Accounts & Item Limits

### No Hacks, X-Ray or Duplication Exploits

Any client, mod, resource pack or exploit that provides an unfair gameplay advantage is strictly forbidden.

Prohibited

- X-Ray packs and mods.

- Hacked clients (for example, Meteor Client).

- Seed crackers.

- Resource locators.

- Item duplication glitches.

**Permitted mods**

- Litematica

- Tweakeroo

Litematica and Tweakeroo are allowed when used strictly within their legitimate building and quality-of-life functionality. Exploiting bugs or abusive features within these mods remains prohibited.

### No Alt Accounts for Advantage or Ban Evasion

- Using alternate accounts to bypass punishments or bans, bypass the Elytra ownership limit, or grant unfair gameplay advantages to your main account is prohibited.

**Exception.** Alt accounts are permitted strictly for AFK or chunk-loading farm operations, provided they comply with the server performance rules.

### No Account Sharing or Selling

- Buying, selling, trading or sharing Minecraft accounts is prohibited.

- You are fully responsible for all actions and rule violations committed on your account.

### Elytra Ownership Limit

Max 3 Elytra

Each player may own and possess a maximum of **3 Elytras** at any given time.

- Hoarding extra Elytras is prohibited.

- Using other players or alts to bypass this limit is prohibited.

**New player gift.** Established new players are eligible to receive **1 free Elytra** from staff after completing **30 days** on the server, subject to staff conditions. This gift still counts toward your 3-Elytra maximum.

## World Preservation & Server Performance

### Do Not Modify Spawn Village

Spawn Village is a protected community hub.

Without explicit staff approval, you must not

- Break, place, obstruct, alter or remove any blocks, structures or decorations within the Spawn area.

Spawn Village is protected. Do not modify it without staff approval.

### Responsible Building, Redstone & Farm Limits

Build creatively, but do not compromise server TPS for everyone else.

- Automated farms and redstone machines must **not** run constantly in unloaded chunks or generate excessive entity or processing lag.

- Maintain reasonable spacing between massive lag-inducing projects.

- If a build degrades server performance, you must cooperate with staff to optimise, disable or remove it.

### Do Not Mine Straight Down

Mining directly downward is strictly prohibited on RenderFrost SMP, to prevent accidental lava and fall deaths and hazardous terrain traps.

- Always use staircase or safe horizontal mining methods.

Claiming a fall was accidental does not excuse a violation.

### Finish Chopping Trees

Keep the landscape clean: always chop down the entire tree and remove all remaining floating logs before moving on.

## Whitelist Access & Rollback Policy

### Whitelist & Player Recommendations

15-day rule

Only authorised staff members can approve and add players to the whitelist.

- **15-day rule:** new players must spend at least **15 days** on RenderFrost SMP from their first login before they can recommend a friend for whitelist consideration.

- Recommended players must still complete the official verification process; meeting the 15-day requirement does not guarantee approval.

### Unified Rollback Policy (World & Inventory)

Not guaranteed

Rollbacks and inventory restorations are **not** automatic guarantees. They are considered **only** when the loss is directly caused by a verified server-side issue, or by confirmed griefing with sufficient staff evidence.

Potentially eligible

- Verified server crashes.

- Severe server lag directly causing the loss.

- Unexpected server-side disconnections.

- Staff-confirmed griefing.

Not eligible

- Normal gameplay deaths.

- Accidental falls or lava deaths.

- Hostile mob or entity damage.

- Ordinary item loss unrelated to a proven technical failure.

To request a review, submit a ticket explaining what happened, when it occurred, and any supporting evidence.

## Community Conduct & Etiquette

### Official Discord server announcement

To maintain a respectful, dignified and welcoming environment for all
members of the Discord server, the administration enforces strict guidelines
on language and shared media. They are effective immediately, and adherence
is mandatory for every member.

1. Zero tolerance for profanity & cuss words

- **Respect for seniors & women:** using cuss words, profanity or vulgar language in the presence of fellow seniors or women is strictly prohibited across all text and voice channels.

- **General decorum:** all members are expected to uphold a high standard of respectful communication at all times.

2. Prohibition of inappropriate content & entertainment

- **No sexism or discrimination:** any form of sexist remarks, gender-based disparagement or disrespectful humour is forbidden.

- **No sexually explicit material:** sharing sexually explicit jokes, suggestive GIFs, NSFW media or any similar form of inappropriate entertainment is strictly disallowed.

Enforcement & consequences

**Violations of these rules will be taken seriously.** Any
member found disregarding these standards will face immediate moderation
action, including formal warnings, temporary mutes or permanent removal
from the server.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping this community respectful and
safe for everyone.
