    How someone becomes a member

    Nobody gets in by finding the address and typing it. Access runs through staff, in a fixed order.

    1. A staff member is contacted and told who wants to join.
    2. The prospective player completes the required verification process.
    3. Staff review the application and make the decision.
    4. Only then is whitelist access granted, and only by staff.

    Players cannot add each other. Independently authorising access, arranging it yourself, or assuming that an invitation is the same as approval are all outside the process — and the staff team keeps final authority over every application.

    Membership at a glance

    • AccessWhitelist only

    After 15 days

    Once you have been on the server for 15 days from your first login, you can ask staff to consider someone you would like to invite. They still go through verification, and meeting the waiting period does not guarantee approval.

    After 30 days

    Established members can receive one Elytra as a gift from the staff team, provided the conditions they set are met. It never overrides the three-Elytra ownership limit.

    What you sign up for

    Membership is a set of obligations, not just a login.

    01

    Respect property

    Every base, farm and chest belongs to someone who expects to find it untouched. Griefing and theft are prohibited outright and investigated when reported.

    02

    Keep consent central

    PvP is off by default. Nobody is attacked, trapped or endangered without agreeing to it, and agreed fights are left alone by everyone else.

    03

    Play fairly

    No hacked clients, X-Ray, seed crackers, resource locators or duplication. Litematica and Tweakeroo are fine when used responsibly.

    04

    Build for the server

    Ambitious projects are welcome. Farms and redstone that drag the whole server down are not, and large builds need sensible spacing.

    05

    Leave shared space alone

    Spawn Village is protected, and staff-only storage halls are strictly off-limits — even when the door is open.

    06

    Cooperate with staff

    Reports are reviewed on evidence and circumstance. Members are expected to cooperate and follow reasonable instructions about safety and fair play.
