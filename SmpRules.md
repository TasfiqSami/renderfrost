# ❄️ The RenderFrost SMP — Official Rulebook ❄️

*Welcome to RenderFrost SMP! Our community is built on trust, fairness, creativity, and respect. Please read all rules carefully before playing.*

---

## 🛡️ I. Community Conduct & Safety

### 01. Be Kind & Respectful
Every player deserves to feel welcome and respected within the RenderFrost community.
- **Prohibited:** Hate speech, discriminatory remarks, slurs, harassment, targeted abuse, deliberate humiliation, excessive vulgarity, or conduct intended to make another player's experience hostile.
- Disagreements are normal, but personal attacks are strictly unacceptable.

### 02. Protect Personal Information
- **Strictly Prohibited:** Doxxing, sharing another player's private/real-world details, or threatening real-world harm.
- **Penalty:** Immediate permanent ban.

### 03. No Advertising or Spam
- Do not advertise other servers, websites, or external services in chat, signs, or books.
- Repeated chat spam, flooding, or disruptive messaging will result in a mute.

### 04. Respect the Staff's Decisions
- Moderation decisions are final.
- If you wish to contest a punishment, submit an appeal through the official support/ticket channel. Arguing in public chat or harassing staff will result in additional penalties.

---

## ⚔️ II. Property, Trading & Combat

### 05. No Griefing or Stealing
Every player's property, builds, and possessions must be respected—even if a base is unattended or a container is unlocked.
- **Strictly Prohibited:** Breaking, modifying, or damaging another player's blocks, builds, farms, or infrastructure without permission, as well as stealing items from chests, barrels, shulker boxes, or other storage.

### 06. Respect Private Storage & Restricted Areas
- Do not enter another player's private storage room without explicit permission.
- **Staff-Only Areas:** Entering staff-only storage halls, accessing restricted containers, or taking staff materials is strictly prohibited and treated as severe griefing/theft.

### 07. No Scamming or Fraudulent Trading
- Scamming other players in trades, player shops, or item exchanges is strictly prohibited.
- All trades are considered final once both parties confirm the exchange.

### 08. No Unconsented PvP or Player Harm
PvP is disabled by default and permitted **only** when all involved players explicitly agree.
- **Prohibited:** Attacking players without consent, trapping players in enclosed spaces, using lava/dripstone/hazards to endanger others, or engineering "accidental" deaths.
- Consent to one fight does not grant permission for future or unrelated attacks.

### 09. Do Not Interrupt Ongoing Friendly PvP
- When players are engaged in an agreed-upon friendly PvP session, do not run into the fight, obstruct participants, or distract them.
- **Penalty:** Intentional disruption may result in several hours of jail time.

### 10. Limits on Explosives & Channeling Tridents
- **Explosives:** TNT, creepers, and end crystals must **not** be used near other players' builds or the Spawn area—even during consent-based PvP.
- **Channeling Tridents:** Do not use Channeling-enchanted tridents near another player's base or structures during thunderstorms. Damage caused "as a joke" is treated as griefing and is not eligible for staff repair.

---

## ⚖️ III. Fair Play, Accounts & Item Limits

### 11. No Hacks, X-Ray, or Duplication Exploits
Any client, mod, resource pack, or exploit that provides an unfair gameplay advantage is strictly forbidden.
- **Prohibited:** X-Ray packs/mods, hacked clients (e.g., Meteor Client), seed crackers, resource locators, and item duplication glitches.
- **Permitted Mods:** **Litematica** and **Tweakeroo** are allowed when used strictly within their legitimate building/QoL functionality. Exploiting bugs or abusive features within these mods remains prohibited.

### 12. No Alt Accounts for Advantage or Ban Evasion
- Using alternate accounts to bypass punishments/bans, bypass the Elytra ownership limit, or grant unfair gameplay advantages to your main account is prohibited.
- **Exception:** Alt accounts are permitted strictly for AFK/chunk-loading farm operations, provided they comply with server performance rules.

### 13. No Account Sharing or Selling
- Buying, selling, trading, or sharing Minecraft accounts is prohibited.
- You are fully responsible for all actions and rule violations committed on your account.

### 14. Elytra Ownership Limit
- Each player may own and possess a maximum of **3 Elytras** at any given time.
- Hoarding extra Elytras or using other players/alts to bypass this limit is prohibited.
- **New Player Gift:** Established new players are eligible to receive **1 free Elytra** from staff after completing **30 days** on the server (subject to staff conditions). This gift still counts toward your 3-Elytra maximum.

---

## 🌍 IV. World Preservation & Server Performance

### 15. Do Not Modify Spawn Village
Spawn Village is a protected community hub. Without explicit staff approval, you must **not**:
- Break, place, obstruct, alter, or remove any blocks, structures, or decorations within the Spawn area.

### 16. Responsible Building, Redstone & Farm Limits
Build creatively, but do not compromise server TPS for everyone else.
- Automated farms and redstone machines must **not** run constantly in unloaded chunks or generate excessive entity/processing lag.
- Maintain reasonable spacing between massive lag-inducing projects.
- If a build degrades server performance, you must cooperate with staff to optimize, disable, or remove it.

### 17. Do Not Mine Straight Down
- Mining directly downward is strictly prohibited on RenderFrost SMP to prevent accidental lava/fall deaths and hazardous terrain traps.
- Always use staircase or safe horizontal mining methods. Claiming a fall was accidental does not excuse a violation.

### 18. Finish Chopping Trees
- Keep the landscape clean: always chop down the entire tree and remove all remaining floating logs before moving on.

---

## 📋 V. Whitelist Access & Rollback Policy

### 19. Whitelist & Player Recommendations
Only authorized staff members can approve and add players to the whitelist.
- **15-Day Rule:** New players must spend at least **15 days** on RenderFrost SMP from their first login before they can recommend a friend for whitelist consideration.
- Recommended players must still complete the official verification process; meeting the 15-day requirement does not guarantee approval.

### 20. Unified Rollback Policy (World & Inventory)
Rollbacks and inventory restorations are **not** automatic guarantees and are considered **only** when loss is directly caused by a verified server-side issue (or confirmed griefing with sufficient staff evidence).
> ✅ **Potentially Eligible:** Verified server crashes, severe server lag directly causing the loss, unexpected server-side disconnections, or staff-confirmed griefing.
> ❌ **Not Eligible:** Normal gameplay deaths, accidental falls/lava deaths, hostile mob/entity damage, or ordinary item loss unrelated to a proven technical failure.
- To request a review, submit a ticket explaining what happened, when it occurred, and any supporting evidence.

---

> **📌 Final Reminder:** *Ignorance of the rules is not an excuse. The RenderFrost staff team reserves the right to investigate incidents and take appropriate action against any behavior that harms the community, even if not explicitly listed above. Respect the world, respect the players, and enjoy the adventure!*
