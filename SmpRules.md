\# 📜 RenderFrost SMP

\## Official Rules \& Regulations



\*Welcome to RenderFrost SMP! Our goal is to maintain a fair, respectful, safe, and enjoyable Minecraft community where everyone can build, explore, and play without unnecessary interference.\*



All players are expected to read, understand, and follow the rules below. By joining and playing on RenderFrost SMP, you agree to comply with these regulations and cooperate with the staff team.



\---



\## 01. 🤝 Be Kind \& Respectful



Every player deserves to feel welcome and respected within the RenderFrost community.



\*\*The following behaviours are prohibited:\*\*

\- Hate speech, discriminatory remarks, and slurs.

\- Harassment, targeted abuse, or deliberately humiliating other players.

\- Excessive vulgarity or intentionally offensive behaviour directed at others.

\- Any conduct intended to make another player's experience uncomfortable or hostile.



Normal conversations are not actively monitored. However, reported incidents involving abusive or inappropriate behaviour will be taken seriously and reviewed by the moderation team.



\*\*Remember:\*\* Disagreements are normal, but personal attacks and harassment are not acceptable.



\---



\## 02. 🛑 No Griefing or Stealing



Every player's property, builds, and possessions must be respected.



\*\*Strictly prohibited actions include:\*\*

\- Breaking, removing, or modifying another player's blocks without permission.

\- Stealing items from another player's chests, barrels, shulker boxes, or other storage.

\- Deliberately damaging or destroying another player's base, farm, infrastructure, or decorations.

\- Tampering with another player's builds or property to cause damage, inconvenience, or disruption.



RenderFrost takes reported griefing and theft seriously. Incidents may be investigated using available evidence, and confirmed violations may result in appropriate moderation action.



\*\*🛑 If you grief or steal, you will be held accountable.\*\*



Do not assume that an unattended base or accessible container means you have permission to use or take anything from it.



\---



\## 03. ⚔️ No Unconsented PvP or Player Harm



Player-versus-player combat is disabled by default. No player should be attacked, trapped, or deliberately placed in a harmful situation without their consent.



\*\*Prohibited behaviour includes:\*\*

\- Attempting to kill or damage another player without their agreement.

\- Trapping players in enclosed spaces or deliberately preventing them from escaping.

\- Using lava, dripstone, or similar hazards to attack or endanger another player.

\- Creating traps intended to cause another player's death or destroy their equipment.

\- Deliberately engineering situations that cause harm while attempting to disguise them as accidents.



PvP is permitted only when the players involved have agreed to participate. Consent to one fight or activity does not automatically grant permission for unrelated attacks or interference.



Reported violations may be investigated and punished accordingly.



\---



\## 04. 🚫 No Hacks, X-Ray, or Duplication Exploits



RenderFrost requires fair gameplay. Any client, modification, resource pack, tool, or exploit that provides an unfair advantage is prohibited.



\*\*❌ Prohibited examples include:\*\*

\- X-Ray resource packs or modifications that reveal hidden ores or other normally concealed resources.

\- Meteor Client or other hacked clients and cheating tools.

\- Seed crackers used to gain an unauthorized gameplay advantage.

\- Resource locators or similar tools that reveal hidden resources or locations unfairly.

\- Item duplication exploits or methods used to duplicate items through unintended game behaviour.

\- Any other modification or exploit that bypasses intended gameplay mechanics to gain an unfair advantage.



\*\*✅ Permitted modifications:\*\*

\- Litematica.

\- Tweakaroo.



Permitted modifications must be used responsibly and within their legitimate functionality. Their names alone do not authorize every possible use; any feature used to cheat or gain an unfair advantage remains prohibited.



Players must not exploit bugs or unintended mechanics to bypass these rules.



\---



\## 05. 🔄 Rollback Policy — World Destruction



A rollback is a restoration of affected blocks or world changes. It is not an automatic guarantee of compensation.



Rollbacks for world destruction will be considered primarily when the damage or loss was directly caused by a \*\*verified server-side issue\*\*, such as:

\- Severe server lag or a server malfunction.

\- An unexpected server crash.

\- A confirmed server-related disconnection that resulted in the loss or destruction of world data.



\*\*Confirmed griefing may also be reviewed for a possible rollback\*\* when sufficient evidence is available and staff determine that restoration is appropriate.



However:

\- Normal player-caused destruction is not automatically eligible for restoration.

\- Natural or entity-caused destruction is not eligible for compensation under the normal rollback policy.

\- A report or request does not guarantee that a rollback will be approved.



The staff team will evaluate the circumstances and available evidence before making a decision.



\---



\## 06. 🎒 Rollback Policy — Inventory Loss



Inventory restoration is considered only when the loss was directly caused by a verified server-side problem.



\*\*Potentially eligible situations include:\*\*

\- Severe server lag directly causing the loss.

\- A server crash that results in an inventory loss.

\- An unexpected server-related disconnection that directly causes items to become irrecoverable.



\*\*The following situations are not eligible for inventory restoration:\*\*

\- Normal deaths during gameplay.

\- Accidental deaths, including falls, lava incidents, or other player mistakes.

\- Deaths caused by hostile mobs or other entities under normal gameplay conditions.

\- Ordinary item loss unrelated to a verified server-side issue.



A death occurring while the server is lagging does not automatically prove that the server caused the loss. The connection between the technical issue and the lost inventory must be established as clearly as possible.



If you believe your situation qualifies, submit a report to the staff team explaining what happened and when it occurred. Include any available evidence.



\*\*Final decisions regarding inventory restoration are made by the staff team after reviewing the incident.\*\*



\---



\## 07. ⛏️ Do Not Mine Straight Down



Mining directly downward is prohibited on RenderFrost SMP.



This practice can lead to unexpected falls, lava exposure, accidental deaths, and dangerous situations involving other players.



\*\*Players must not mine straight down, even when they believe the area below them is safe.\*\*



Use a proper mining method that allows you to inspect the blocks and terrain ahead of you.



This is a punishable offense. Claiming that the fall or damage was accidental does not automatically excuse a violation of this rule.



\---



\## 08. 🌳 Finish Chopping Trees



Keep the world clean and maintain the natural appearance of RenderFrost SMP.



Whenever you chop down a tree, remove the remaining logs rather than leaving a partially chopped trunk floating above the ground.



\*\*Players are expected to:\*\*

\- Finish cutting down the entire tree.

\- Remove any remaining exposed logs.

\- Avoid leaving unfinished trees that unnecessarily disrupt the surrounding environment.



This rule helps preserve the server's appearance and makes the world more enjoyable for everyone.



🌿 \*\*If you start chopping a tree, finish the job!\*\*



\---



\## 09. 🏡 Do Not Modify Spawn Village



The Spawn Village is a protected area that serves as an important shared location for the RenderFrost community.



\*\*Without explicit permission from the staff team, you must not:\*\*

\- Break, remove, or damage existing blocks or structures.

\- Place blocks that modify or obstruct the protected area.

\- Build over, alter, or replace existing structures.

\- Remove decorations or modify the village's appearance.

\- Make any other changes to the spawn environment.



Even seemingly minor modifications can affect the shared appearance and functionality of the area.



If you believe a change is necessary, contact a staff member and obtain permission before proceeding.



\*\*Spawn Village is protected. Do not modify it without staff approval.\*\*



\---



\## 10. 🪂 Elytra Ownership Limit



Each player may own a maximum of \*\*3 Elytras\*\* on RenderFrost SMP.



This limit is intended to prevent excessive accumulation and preserve a reasonable distribution of Elytras throughout the community.



\*\*The following restrictions apply:\*\*

\- You may not keep more than three Elytras in your possession.

\- You may not hoard additional Elytras beyond the permitted limit.

\- You may not trade or transfer extra Elytras in a way that bypasses the ownership restriction.



\### 🎁 New Player Elytra Gift



New players are eligible to receive \*\*one Elytra after completing 30 days on the server\*\*, provided they meet the applicable conditions set by the staff team.



This is a gift from the staff team to welcome and reward established members of the community.



The 30-day period does not grant permission to bypass the three-Elytra ownership limit.



\---



\## 11. ⚡ Channeling Trident Restrictions



Channeling-enchanted tridents can cause lightning strikes during suitable thunderstorms. Using them carelessly around important structures can create unnecessary damage or disruption.



Players must avoid using Channeling tridents near another player's base, house, or other important structure when doing so could cause damage or disturbance.



\*\*Prohibited behaviour includes:\*\*

\- Deliberately using Channeling to damage or disrupt another player's property.

\- Intentionally causing damage and dismissing it as a joke.

\- Using Channeling to provoke other players or interfere with their builds.



Damage caused as a joke is not automatically eligible for repair or rollback.



If the staff team confirms that the incident involved intentional griefing, appropriate moderation action may be taken under the server's rules.



\*\*Always consider the potential consequences before using Channeling near another player's property.\*\*



\---



\## 12. 🏗️ Responsible Building \& Server Performance



RenderFrost encourages creativity, ambitious projects, and impressive builds. However, everyone must build responsibly and consider the effect their constructions may have on server performance.



Players should avoid creating excessively resource-intensive structures that cause unnecessary lag or negatively affect the gameplay experience of others.



\*\*Examples of builds that may require attention include:\*\*

\- Excessively lag-inducing automatic farms.

\- Overly large or inefficient redstone systems.

\- Machines or mechanisms that generate excessive entity or processing load.

\- Other constructions that significantly degrade server performance.



Large projects should be designed and maintained with reasonable performance in mind.



Players should also maintain a reasonable distance between large builds when necessary to reduce interference, preserve the surrounding environment, and support server performance.



If a build is found to be causing significant problems, cooperate with the staff team to address the issue.



\*\*Build creatively, but do not compromise the experience of the entire server.\*\*



\---



\## 13. 🔐 Whitelist \& Player Access



RenderFrost SMP operates with a whitelist system to maintain a trusted and controlled community.



\*\*Only authorized staff members can officially add players to the whitelist.\*\*



Players must not independently add, attempt to authorize, or arrange unauthorized access for other individuals.



\### Want a Friend to Join?



Follow the official process:

1\. Contact a staff member and explain whom you would like to invite.

2\. Ensure the prospective player follows the required verification process.

3\. Wait for staff approval and official whitelist access.



Do not assume that sharing server information or receiving a personal invitation automatically authorizes someone to join.



All whitelist additions are subject to staff verification and approval.



\---



\## 14. 📦 Respect Private Storage \& Restricted Areas



Every player's private storage and designated restricted areas must be respected.



\*\*You must not enter another player's private storage area without permission.\*\*



This includes accessing, interfering with, or taking items from their storage containers without authorization.



\### Staff-Only Storage Halls



Staff-only storage halls are strictly restricted to authorized staff members.



Players must not:

\- Enter restricted storage halls without authorization.

\- Access or remove items from staff storage.

\- Interfere with stored materials, organized resources, or restricted equipment.

\- Use unauthorized access to obtain items or information.



Unauthorized access to another player's storage, taking their belongings, or interfering with their property may be treated as \*\*griefing or stealing\*\*, depending on the circumstances.



Respect all private and restricted areas, even when access appears physically possible.



\---



\## 15. 🥊 Do Not Interrupt Ongoing Friendly PvP



When players are participating in an agreed-upon friendly PvP session, allow them to complete their fight without unnecessary interference.



\*\*You do not need to participate, fight, or provide assistance. Simply respect the ongoing activity.\*\*



Players must not deliberately:

\- Run into the fight to disrupt the participants.

\- Interfere with the fight or distract those involved.

\- Obstruct the participants or otherwise prevent the agreed-upon session from continuing normally.



This rule applies to intentional disruption; merely being nearby or passing through is not automatically a violation.



⚠️ \*\*Intentionally disrupting an ongoing friendly PvP session may result in several hours of jail time.\*\*



Respect the participants and give them space to finish their fight.



\---



\## 16. 👥 Player Recommendations \& Invitation Restrictions



New players must spend at least \*\*15 days on RenderFrost SMP from their first login\*\* before they can recommend another player for whitelist consideration.



This waiting period allows the staff team to become familiar with new members, establish trust, and maintain the quality and security of the community.



\### How the 15-Day Requirement Works



\- Before completing 15 days, you cannot recommend or request another player's addition based on your invitation.

\- After completing the required period, you may request that another player be considered for the whitelist.

\- The recommended player must still complete the required verification process.

\- Meeting the 15-day requirement does not guarantee approval.



\*\*The staff team retains final authority over whitelist applications and player access.\*\*



Please respect this requirement and allow the staff team to complete its verification process.
